ChromaSol for iOS, version 1.0.0 · Effective 22 September 2026

How it works

ChromaSol looks at a photo, isolates the people, leaves skin out and reduces the clothing to its true colors. It then searches the 348 combinations of Sanzo Wada's dictionary for the closest one.

FAQ

Do I need a connection? No. The app never uses the network.

Why does it find no people in a photo? The crop uses Apple Vision; if nobody is recognized, the whole photo is analyzed. You can change this in Settings > Crop.

Which languages? English, Spanish and German, following the iPhone's language.
